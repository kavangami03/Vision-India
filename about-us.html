About Us

Vision Embesoft Solution (VES)

Founded in 2008, Vision Embesoft Solution (VES) is a leading manufacturer, developer, and solution provider of laser systems, machine vision solutions, and industrial automation equipment, headquartered in Gandhinagar, Gujarat, India.

At VES, we are committed to empowering industries through innovation, automation, and precision engineering. We understand that every sector has its own challenges, and our team delivers customized, high-performance solutions that enhance productivity, quality, and consistency.

Our comprehensive product range includes:

  • High-Speed Auto Loading Camera-Based Inspection, Measurement & Sorting Machines
  • Automation for Laser Marking, Laser Welding, and Laser Cutting Systems
  • Laser Diamond Cutting Machines
  • Special Purpose Automation Machines (SPM)

These cutting-edge systems are designed to serve diverse industries such as engineering, electronics, automotive, jewelry, medical devices, packaging, and semiconductors.

With a strong foundation in research, design, and innovation, Vision Embesoft Solution (VES) continues to set new benchmarks in laser technology, automation, and intelligent vision inspection, helping industries achieve greater efficiency, precision, and reliability.
